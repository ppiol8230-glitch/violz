Contact & visit

Atelier VIOLZ — Suwon, Korea. Visitors always welcome.

Visitors are always welcome. Whether you are thinking of commissioning an instrument, are simply curious about early strings, or would like nothing more than a look around — any reason is a good one. The phone is hard to answer while work is under way, so please get in touch before you come.
